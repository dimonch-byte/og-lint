og:image not showing on LinkedIn, Slack or X?

Paste the <head> of your page (View Source → copy from <head> to </head>). This lint checks the markup only and runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

What this can't see

A markup lint can't tell you what a crawler actually received. The most common non-markup causes: the bot is blocked (Cloudflare Bot Fight Mode / WAF returning 403 to facebookexternalhit or LinkedInBot), the image URL redirects or 404s, the image is SVG/WebP-only or too large, or the platform is showing a cached preview (re-scrape in LinkedIn Post Inspector / Facebook Sharing Debugger).
